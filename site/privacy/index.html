NoChords

Privacy

Last updated 10 October 2026

NoChords has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It keeps your songs, and only where you put them.

Who

NoChords is made by Anton Kulikov. Questions about this page, or about your data, go to help@nochords.app.

Without an account

Your songs and your settings — the theme, the metronome, the count-in — are kept in your browser's storage on your device. They are not sent anywhere. Clearing the site's data, or removing the app, deletes them.

With an account

Signing in is optional, and only there so your songs can follow you between devices.

  • Signing in uses your Google account through Firebase Authentication, a Google service. It receives your Google account's name, email address and profile photo link, and gives your account an ID.
  • Your songs are stored in Cloud Firestore, also Google's, under that ID. Only you can read or change them: the database refuses every other request, including anyone else's account.
  • Nothing else is stored about you, and nothing is shared, sold or used for advertising.

Hosting

The web version of the app is served by Firebase Hosting, and this site by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, they see the IP address and request of each visit in order to deliver the page, under their own privacy policies. NoChords does not use that information to identify or follow you. The iOS and Android apps carry their own files, and reach out only to sign you in and to keep your songs in your account.

The app's fonts are served with it, not from a font service. Links out — to GitHub, to Buy Me a Coffee — lead to sites with privacy policies of their own.

Email

Mail to help@nochords.app is forwarded by Cloudflare to the maker's own mailbox, and used only to answer you.

Deleting your data

  • A song you delete in the app is deleted from your device, and from your account if you are signed in.
  • To delete your account and everything stored with it, open the account button at the top of your songs and choose Delete account. You sign in once more to confirm, and the account and its songs are deleted for good. If you cannot reach the app, write to help@nochords.app from the address you sign in with, and it will be deleted.
  • Signing out keeps your account and its songs; it only stops this device using them.

Changes

If this page changes, the new version is posted here with its date.
